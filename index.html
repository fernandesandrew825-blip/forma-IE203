11 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Assignment 1 project summary.

FORMA Smart Modular Furniture is a virtual smart-manufacturing company that combines computer graphics, modular product design, CNC manufacturing, IIoT technology, Visual Factory Management, and Lean and Green Industrial Engineering.

Customers digitally configure furniture based on their space and requirements. Computer graphics and CAD/CAM transform those selections into visual product models and manufacturing information for CNC-controlled equipment.

Connected machines and sensors provide factory-performance information. A Visual Factory system makes machine status, production progress, material utilization, defects, waste, energy consumption, and other key information visible to workers and managers.

Lean principles reduce production waste and improve flow, while Green Engineering focuses on material efficiency, energy consumption, product longevity, repairability, reuse, and recycling.

Through collaboration with Autodesk, Siemens, Rockwell Automation, and IKEA, FORMA demonstrates how Computer Graphics in Industrial Engineering can connect design, manufacturing, information, sustainability, and continuous improvement within a modern smart factory.
